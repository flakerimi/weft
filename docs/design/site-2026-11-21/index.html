Components that know whether they're under a finger or a mouse, on a landing page or in an app. Your site and your API ship as one Go binary.

$ curl -fsSL https://weft.base.al/install | sh

Write a component once. It adapts.

You write <DatePicker>. On a trackpad it opens a calendar beside the field; under a thumb it rises as a sheet with targets big enough to hit. No mobile variant to remember.

<!-- pages/booking.vue -->
<Page title="Book a visit">
  <DatePicker
    v-model="day"
    label="Day"
  />
</Page>

Requests per second, median of three runs on an Apple M4. Part of the gap is the server: Weft sends brotli it made at build time, Astro's servers compress each response. Astro's plain-script page still ships less JavaScript than ours, and we say where in the benchmark.

Made to be built with an agent.

Agents do best with big pieces and one right way. Weft gives them both, and tells them when they're wrong before you see it.

  • A catalogue they can readEvery component's props, slots and how it adapts, as JSON, from weft catalog or weft mcp.
  • Mistakes fail the buildWrong frontmatter, a missing env value or an off-system <button> stops the build at the file and line.
  • Skills, ready to installBuilding a page, adding a CRUD screen, theming, live data from Go.
